# User Profiles

Ordered from most likely to use the app to least likely.

## 1. Ravi, the Syndication Builder

- Role: creates a syndicated marketplace and wants to connect multiple sources into one usable experience.
- Why he fits: the app is built around syndicated endpoints, marketplace discovery, and a multi-source publishing model.
- Main goals: bring multiple marketplaces together, keep the source list healthy, and make the network feel unified.
- Likely behavior: sets up endpoints, checks whether sources are working, and watches how content flows across the network.

## 2. Mila, the Company Promoter

- Role: promotes a company by posting updates, announcements, and linked content.
- Why she fits: the app gives her a place to publish company visibility content into a networked marketplace.
- Main goals: increase awareness, drive clicks into the company profile, and keep the audience informed.
- Likely behavior: writes posts, links to company pages, and tracks engagement from the community.

## 3. Dorian, the Marketplace Seller

- Role: posts jobs, products, services, or venture listings for a company or community.
- Why he fits: the app gives him a place to publish once and reach multiple connected audiences.
- Main goals: attract attention, generate leads, and keep his listings current.
- Likely behavior: creates and edits listings, shares them, and checks engagement on his content.

## 4. Lena, the Repeat Buyer

- Role: returns to buy products and services from trusted participants.
- Why she fits: she benefits from the cart, order, and payment flow once she already knows the ecosystem.
- Main goals: find relevant offers quickly and complete checkout without friction.
- Likely behavior: browses categories, saves time with familiar sellers, and reorders when possible.

## 5. Tomas, the Curious Explorer

- Role: a new visitor who wants to understand what the network offers.
- Why he fits: he is interested in browsing, reading posts, and testing whether the community is active.
- Main goals: get oriented, compare categories, and decide whether to sign up.
- Likely behavior: scans the homepage, opens a few detail pages, and follows interesting trails.

## 6. Eli, the Hiring Manager

- Role: creates job postings because he is looking to hire someone.
- Why he fits: the app makes it possible to publish jobs into a visible, searchable network.
- Main goals: attract qualified applicants and keep the job listing current.
- Likely behavior: writes a job post, updates the role as hiring needs change, and watches for responses.

## 7. Quinn, the Venture Promoter

- Role: has a venture in mind and wants to promote it through posting and commenting.
- Why he fits: the app lets him build visibility through posts, discussion, and venture-related listings.
- Main goals: share the vision, invite discussion, and grow interest around the venture.
- Likely behavior: posts updates, comments on related content, and uses feedback to refine the idea.

## 8. Petra, the Product Fan

- Role: finds a product she likes and wants to like or comment on it.
- Why she fits: the app supports lightweight engagement even when she is not ready to buy.
- Main goals: signal interest, leave feedback, and follow the product or seller story.
- Likely behavior: opens product details, reacts with likes, and comments on what stands out.

## 9. Nia, the Poor Fit User

- Role: a one-off shopper who expects a standard single-store marketplace with no community context.
- Why she is a poor fit: the app’s multi-endpoint, community-heavy, and crypto-aware model adds more steps than she wants.
- Main goals: none that align strongly with the product unless she adapts to the ecosystem.
- Likely behavior: gets confused by syndication, identity layers, or wallet/payment choices and may leave quickly.
