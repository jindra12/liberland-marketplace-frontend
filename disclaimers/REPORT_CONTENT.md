# Report Content

If something on NSWAP looks wrong, unsafe, misleading, or illegal, you can report it. Reports help us keep the network usable without forcing people to guess where to send concerns.

## What can be reported

Users can report:

- Posts
- Comments
- Listings
- Company pages
- Venture pages
- Identity or tribe pages where relevant
- User profiles where relevant
- Syndication entries or other server-related pages where a source looks unsafe or misleading

Common reasons include:

- Fraud or scam activity
- Illegal goods or services
- Harassment or abuse
- Impersonation
- Copyright or trademark issues
- Spam or repeated abuse
- Misleading or unsafe content

## How to report something

1. Open the page, listing, or comment you want to report
2. Choose the report action
3. Select the reason that fits best
4. Add a short explanation if you want to give more context
5. Send the report

## What happens next

- Your report is saved for review
- The content may stay visible, be limited, be hidden, or be removed depending on the issue
- If the problem belongs to a third-party server, the report may need to be handled there as well
- If we need more information, we may ask for a short follow-up

## Minimum handling standards

- Reports should be easy to find on content pages
- The same reporting options should feel familiar across posts, listings, comments, profiles, and server pages
- We aim to keep the reporter’s identity private where possible
- Obvious fraud or illegal material should be handled quickly

## Practical notes

- Reporting should work on both mobile and desktop
- A short explanation is usually better than a long one
- If a page is from another server, the result may depend on that server’s operator
