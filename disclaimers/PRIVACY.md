# Privacy Notice

NSWAP is a federated frontend that helps people browse and publish marketplace, social, and community content from multiple servers. We keep the data we control as small as possible and use it only to run the service, route users to the right servers, and support the features they choose to use.

## What we collect or receive

Depending on how you use the app, NSWAP may process:

- Account and profile details from your identity provider, such as name, email address, avatar, and verification status
- Public profile information you add in the app, including nickname, contact details, wallets, and shipping information where relevant
- Content you publish, such as posts, comments, likes, subscriptions, listings, company pages, job posts, and venture pages
- Marketplace and order data, including cart contents, order submission details, and shipping details you provide for checkout
- Server and routing data, including the URLs of the servers you add or enable locally
- Support and moderation requests, including data you send when reporting content or requesting help
- Behavior data about the pages you visit and the requests you send while using the app
- Technical data needed to keep the service working, such as logs and basic request metadata

## Why we process it

We use this data to:

- Show the pages and content you asked to view
- Let you create, edit, comment on, like, share, subscribe to, or publish content
- Let you manage profile information, wallets, and contact details
- Route you to the right server when a page or action depends on a specific backend
- Support direct-to-seller purchase flows and order submission
- Review reported content and handle abuse, fraud, or legal issues
- Understand which pages and requests are being used so we can improve the service and spot abuse
- Keep the app secure, reliable, and maintainable

## What NSWAP does not do

- NSWAP does not take possession of payment funds or act as the payment processor for marketplace purchases
- Some marketplace offers may be published by the operator on the main server, while others may come from third-party servers
- NSWAP does not store the full distributed network of content on its own backend
- NSWAP does not use your data for unrelated advertising or unrelated profiling
- NSWAP does not control third-party servers, their availability, or their internal privacy practices
- NSWAP does not use cookies

## Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the data involved, you can ask for:

- Access to the personal data we hold about you
- Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Deletion of data where we are able to delete it
- Export of the data you provided, where applicable
- An explanation of how a particular feature or record is handled

## Retention

We keep data only for as long as needed to operate the service, handle reports, support security, or meet legal obligations. When data is no longer needed, we remove it or stop using it where possible.

## Third-party servers

Many pages in NSWAP load content from servers operated by other people. When you use those pages, the remote server may also process your request and may apply its own privacy terms.

## Contact

If you want to make a privacy request, use the process described in the Data Requests document.

## Updates

We may update this notice when the app changes or when the legal position changes.
